Mission briefing

What is the OSI model?

The Open Systems Interconnection model is a seven-layer reference model. It gives networking tasks separate jobs, making communication easier to understand, design and troubleshoot. Real protocols do not always fit perfectly into one layer, but the model is an excellent thinking tool.
